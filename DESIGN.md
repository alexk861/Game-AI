# Design System: UNCANNY
**Project ID:** Local implementation; Stitch MCP server was not exposed to this Codex session.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
UNCANNY is a cinematic perception experiment disguised as a found internet artifact. The atmosphere is restrained, archival, uneasy, and observational. Screens should feel like states of mind rather than app pages: sparse, tense, and partially unresolved. The interface should recede so the image feels like evidence being inspected.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Archive Black (#131313):** Primary background; creates silence and visual pressure around the image.
- **Burnt Surface (#201f1f):** Panel and overlay surface; used sparingly for reveal and result states.
- **Faded Evidence Text (#cfc4c5):** Muted text, secondary labels, and quiet metadata.
- **Old Instrument Outline (#988e90):** Borders, neutral status, and observed/real decisions.
- **Unstable Red (#a04040):** Synthetic decision, failed perception, warning, and unresolved tension.
- **Dim Mechanical Border (#3a3637):** Structural dividers and low-emphasis panels.

## 3. Typography Rules
Geist is the primary face for restrained body copy. Space Mono is used for archive labels, decisions, timers, result marks, and metadata. Letter spacing is deliberate and clinical on small uppercase labels. Avoid playful, oversized, or marketing-style typography.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Sharp rectangular controls with 0px corner radius, thin outlines, muted fill, and decisive inverted hover states. Buttons should feel binary and heavy, not glossy.
* **Cards/Containers:** Avoid rounded cards and floating panels. When framing is necessary, use flat archival panels with hard edges, low contrast, and no shadows.
* **Inputs/Forms:** Hard-edged, thin-stroked, and quiet. No pill shapes, glow, glass, or gradient treatments.
* **Image Areas:** Full-bleed or dominant within the viewport. Subtle grain, scanlines, compression language, and metadata overlays are allowed when they remain understated.
* **Reveal States:** Quiet and uncomfortable. Use source labels and perception language, never celebration.

## 5. Layout Principles
The image owns the screen, especially on mobile. Whitespace is not emptiness; it is tension. Keep chrome minimal, controls thumb-heavy, and metadata sparse. Avoid dashboards, dense analytics, gamified reward loops, social-feed rhythms, and explanatory UI copy.
