# uncanny-copywriter

## Purpose

This skill defines the official copywriting voice for UNCANNY across the game, website, Google Play listing, onboarding, results screens, reflection flows, legal summaries, and microcopy.

UNCANNY is a daily perception game where players decide whether images are real photographs or AI-generated images.

The copy must make the product feel:
- clear
- premium
- minimal
- human
- mysterious
- playable
- mobile-first

The copy must NOT make the product feel like:
- an AI detector
- a scanner tool
- a forensic dashboard
- a cyberpunk interface
- a scientific lab
- a protocol system
- a generic casual puzzle game

---

## Core One-Sentence Definition

Always preserve this meaning:

“UNCANNY is a daily game where you decide whether images are real or AI.”

This sentence does not need to appear everywhere, but every piece of copy must support this understanding.

---

## Voice Principles

### 1. Clear before clever

The user must understand what to do immediately.

Prefer:
“Choose Real or AI.”

Avoid:
“Classify the perceptual origin of the visual record.”

---

### 2. Premium but not pretentious

UNCANNY should feel refined, not over-written.

Prefer:
“Look closer.”

Avoid:
“Interrogate the synthetic instability beneath the image surface.”

---

### 3. Mysterious but not confusing

A little tension is good.
Confusion is bad.

Prefer:
“Most people got this wrong.”

Avoid:
“The archive failed to stabilize consensus drift.”

---

### 4. Game-first

The product is a game, not a research tool.

Prefer:
“Play today’s set.”

Avoid:
“Begin diagnostic classification.”

---

### 5. Human tone

Use language people understand quickly.

Prefer:
“See how other players answered.”

Avoid:
“Compare against global perceptual consensus telemetry.”

## Gameplay Copy Rules

### Onboarding

Use:

“Can you tell real from AI?”

“Five images.
Twelve seconds each.
A new challenge every day.”

“Choose Real or AI.”

Do not over-explain.

---

### Reveal Screen

Use simple emotional clarity.

Correct:
“Correct.”

Wrong:
“Fooled.”

Timeout:
“Time’s up.”

Then add one short insight:

“Most players missed this one.”

“Only 28% got this right.”

“The details made this harder than it looked.”

Avoid long explanations unless the user expands details.

---

### Results Screen

Use:

“Today’s Results”

Show:
- Score
- Board
- Best fooled image
- Crowd comparison
- Streak
- Share

Avoid:
- report
- debrief
- classification grid
- perception drift as primary label
- diagnostic tone

---

### Reflection / Rewarded Video Copy

Never say:
“Watch ad.”

Use:

“Continue Exploring”

“3 additional images available.”

Disclosure:
“A short sponsor-supported video will play.”

After completion:
“Reflection unlocked.”

Do not use:
- reward
- coins
- bonus life
- free play
- claim
- prize

---

## Landing Page Copy Rules

Homepage must answer within 5 seconds:

“What is this?”

Target answer:
“A daily game where I decide whether images are real or AI.”

Homepage structure:

1. Hero
2. How it works
3. Screenshots
4. Daily habit
5. Download
6. Simple footer

Navigation:
- Play
- How It Works
- Download

Footer only:
- Privacy
- Terms
- Contact
- Google Play Info

No legal or compliance language on homepage.

---

## Google Play Copy Rules

Google Play copy can include ASO keywords naturally:

- AI vs Real
- Real or AI
- Spot AI images
- Daily challenge
- Visual puzzle
- Perception game
- Real or fake

But do not sound like a utility app.

Prefer:
“Can you tell real photographs from AI images?”

Avoid:
“AI detector scanner for image authenticity.”

---

## Microcopy Rules

Loading:
“Preparing today’s set.”

Empty state:
“Today’s set is complete.”

Error:
“Something went wrong. Try again.”

No internet:
“Connection lost. Please try again.”

No account:
“No account needed.”

---

## Copy Length Rules

Hero headline:
Maximum 8 words.

Hero subheadline:
Maximum 3 short lines.

Button:
Maximum 3 words.

Reveal insight:
Maximum 12 words.

Screenshot caption:
Maximum 8 words.

Results paragraph:
Maximum 2 short sentences.

## Final Copy QA Checklist

Before approving any user-facing copy, verify:

1. Can a casual player understand it instantly?
2. Does it sound like a game, not a tool?
3. Is it premium without being pretentious?
4. Is it mysterious without being confusing?
5. Is it free of forbidden technical language?
6. Is it consistent with the rest of UNCANNY?
7. Is it short enough for mobile?
8. Does it help the player act?

If any answer is no, rewrite.
