# Uncanny UX Game-Writing System

## Product Identity
UNCANNY is a fast perception game and social experiment about real vs AI images.

It should feel like an uncanny internet experience, not a lore-heavy ARG or cryptic art project.

## Copy Priorities
1. Clarity
2. Tension
3. Social pressure
4. Light mystery

The player must understand every screen immediately.

## Voice
- Plain
- Short
- Direct
- Slightly unsettling
- Socially aware
- Modern game UX
- Documentary UI

## Rules
1. Use plain language first.
2. Add tension second.
3. Add mystery lightly.
4. Never hide button meaning.
5. Never invent fake technical jargon.
6. Avoid poetic or abstract phrases.
7. Avoid sci-fi exposition.
8. Keep most UI text under one short sentence.

Bad:
- Export residue
- Certainty drift retained
- Contaminated observer archive
- Source integrity unstable

Good:
- Share results
- Most users failed here
- This image was real
- You were fooled
- Human observers disagreed
- People trusted the wrong details

## Onboarding
- Label: Daily challenge
- Title: UNCANNY
- Subtitle: Can you tell what is real?
- CTA: Begin challenge

## Gameplay
- Decision label: Choose
- Real label: Real
- AI label: AI
- Pending state: Checking answer
- Investigation prompt: Hold to look closer

Social pressure examples:
- Most users failed here.
- Human observers disagreed.
- People trusted the wrong details.
- This one caused hesitation.
- Something feels off.
- Confidence dropped here.

## Investigation
Investigation should feel intimate but understandable.

Use:
- EXIF: unknown
- reverse search failed
- compression looks unusual
- details do not settle
- observers disagreed
- look again

## Reveal
- Label: Answer
- Real: This image was real.
- AI: This image was AI.
- Correct: You were right.
- Incorrect: You were fooled.
- Timeout: No answer selected.
- Social line: Most users thought this was AI.

## Results
- Label: Results
- Metric: Accuracy
- Comparison: You scored higher than [x]% of players today.
- Time: Time [m:ss]
- Misleading image: Most misleading image
- Breakdown: Your answers
- Mark labels: Right, Wrong, Skipped

## CTAs
- Share results
- Restart test
- Come back tomorrow.

## Errors
- Challenge unavailable.
- Could not load challenge.
- Connection failed.
- Image failed to load.
- Refresh and try again.
